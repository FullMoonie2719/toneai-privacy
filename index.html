ToneAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 August 2026

ToneAI is an AI-powered writing and reply suggestion application. This Privacy Policy explains what information ToneAI collects, how it is used, where it may be processed, and how you can delete your account.

1. Who we are

ToneAI is the name of the application. For privacy questions, contact scotthall632@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication information.
  • Profile information and a profile photograph if you choose to provide one.
  • Text that you voluntarily submit for AI-powered suggestions or writing assistance.
  • AI-generated suggestions and related application activity needed to provide the service.
  • Analytics information, where analytics is enabled, such as app events and feature usage.

ToneAI's current Android release does not request SMS or contact permissions.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide AI-powered writing and reply suggestions.
  • To save suggestion history and profile information you choose to keep.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the application.
  • To understand feature usage and application performance where analytics is enabled.
  • To process account deletion requests and remove associated account data.

4. AI processing and third parties

When you use AI features, the text required to generate a response is sent through ToneAI's backend infrastructure to the AI provider selected by the service. ToneAI is designed to keep provider API credentials on the server rather than inside the mobile application.

ToneAI may use OpenAI and Perplexity for AI processing. The applicable provider may process submitted content under its own terms and privacy practices. ToneAI does not sell your personal information.

ToneAI may also use Supabase for authentication, database services, storage and backend functions, and Mixpanel for analytics when analytics is enabled.

5. Analytics

ToneAI may use Mixpanel to understand application usage and feature interactions. Analytics may include events such as suggestions viewed or approved and relevant settings or connection states. Analytics is used for product improvement and service operation.

6. Data security

ToneAI uses reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including encrypted connections for data transmitted between the app and backend services. Provider API credentials are intended to remain server-side and are not stored in the mobile application.

7. Data retention

We retain account and service data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Data that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymised.

8. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your ToneAI account from the account/settings area of the application. ToneAI also provides an account-deletion mechanism through its backend. When an account deletion request is completed, associated account data is intended to be permanently deleted, subject to information that must be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

If you cannot access the app, contact scotthall632@gmail.com for assistance with account deletion.

9. Children's privacy

ToneAI is not directed to children under the applicable minimum age for using the service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ToneAI's features, services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact scotthall632@gmail.com.
